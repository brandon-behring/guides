# About

This site hosts public, open-access interview-prep guides for data science, ML engineering, and AI engineering roles (target audience: mid-to-senior IC candidates, L4–L6 equivalent). Per-guide content lives in sibling repos and deploys to subroutes of this hub.

## Author

**Brandon Behring** — applied math PhD (2022), data scientist, currently working toward an Anthropic 1-year arc (Fellows / Research Engineer / Alignment Science). These guides are a career-bridge artifact: they let me crystallize material I work with, demonstrate AI-assisted authoring methodology at scale, and contribute the kind of compact, equation-fluent technical writing that doesn't yet exist for this audience across the open web.
