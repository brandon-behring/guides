## A3.1 Pictorial Illustrations Still Improve Students' Learning from Text

- **Pictorial Illustrations Still Improve Students' Learning from Text** — Carney & Levin (2002); *Educational Psychology Review, 14(1), 5-26*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1013176309260
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reviews 1970s-90s research supporting that carefully constructed text illustrations enhance learners performance on text-dependent cognitive outcomes, and begins to systematically explore the whys, whens, and for-whoms of picture facilitation.
  - **Result:** Most-cited review of picture-in-text effects with explicit dual-coding framing; load-bearing for any claim that illustrations help comprehension of technical prose.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0039

## A3.2 Dual Coding Theory and Education

- **Dual Coding Theory and Education** — Clark & Paivio (1991); *Educational Psychology Review, 3(3), 149-210*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01320076
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Dual coding theory (DCT) explains human behavior in terms of dynamic associative processes that operate on a rich network of modality-specific verbal and nonverbal (or imagery) representations; shows how DCT mechanisms can be used to model diverse educational phenomena.
  - **Result:** Canonical Paivio-co-authored treatment of dual coding applied to instructional design; load-bearing pre-1991 statement most often cited alongside paivio1991dual in educational contexts.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0036

## A3.3 Increasing word distinctiveness eliminates the picture superiority effect in recognition: Evidence for the physical-distinctiveness account

- **Increasing word distinctiveness eliminates the picture superiority effect in recognition: Evidence for the physical-distinctiveness account** — Ensor, Surprenant & Neath (2019); *Memory & Cognition, 47, 182-193*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-018-0858-9
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Manipulating physical distinctiveness of words eliminates the picture-superiority effect, supporting a distinctiveness-based rather than dual-coding-based mechanism for the picture advantage in recognition memory.
  - **Result:** Memory & Cognition primary study (high-cited) underpinning the distinctiveness critique of DCT picture-superiority explanations.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0041

## A3.4 Distinctiveness, not dual coding, explains the picture-superiority effect

- **Distinctiveness, not dual coding, explains the picture-superiority effect** — Higdon, Neath, Surprenant & Ensor (2025); *Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology (2025)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218241235520
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Experimental results are consistent with the physical-distinctiveness account and inconsistent with dual-coding theory as the explanation for the picture-superiority effect — i.e., picture advantage may be a distinctiveness phenomenon, not a dual-encoding one.
  - **Result:** Critical counterweight: shows the contemporary debate over whether dual coding mechanistically explains picture-superiority, important for not over-claiming DCT in the dossier.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0040

## A3.6 Mind and Its Evolution: A Dual Coding Theoretical Approach

- **Mind and Its Evolution: A Dual Coding Theoretical Approach** — Paivio (2007); *Lawrence Erlbaum Associates*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://www.routledge.com/Mind-and-Its-Evolution-A-Dual-Coding-Theoretical-Approach/Paivio/p/book/9780805852608
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Updates the Dual Coding Theory of mind, a theory of modern human cognition consisting of separate but interconnected nonverbal and verbal systems, and carefully compares DCT with contrasting single-code cognitive theories.
  - **Result:** Paivio mature/post-1991 restatement of DCT — useful as the citation that supersedes paivio1991dual for theoretical claims about the architecture itself.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0038

## A3.7 Imagery and Text: A Dual Coding Theory of Reading and Writing (2nd ed.)

- **Imagery and Text: A Dual Coding Theory of Reading and Writing (2nd ed.)** — Sadoski & Paivio (2013); *Routledge (2nd ed., orig. 2001)*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://www.routledge.com/Imagery-and-Text-A-Dual-Coding-Theory-of-Reading-and-Writing/Sadoski-Paivio/p/book/9780415898485
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** First book to take a systematic dual-coding theoretical approach to all central issues of literacy — decoding, comprehension, memory in reading; planning, drafting, reviewing in writing — extending DCT to text-based learning.
  - **Result:** Bridges dual-coding theory directly to text/picture coordination in reading and writing; high-cited in textbook-design and technical-writing pedagogy literatures.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0037
