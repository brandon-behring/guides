Sub-area `A1` of the Research Plan: Multi-paradigm presentation + UDL (Universal Design for Learning) dossier. 8 entries.

## A1.1 Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A Content Analysis of Peer-Reviewed Journal Papers from 2012 to 2015

- **Universal Design for Learning (UDL): A Content Analysis of Peer-Reviewed Journal Papers from 2012 to 2015** — Al-Azawei, Serenelli & Lundqvist (2016); *Journal of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning, 16(3)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1104867
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Content analysis of 12 peer-reviewed empirical UDL papers (2012-2015); finds UDL research is in nascent stages with inconsistent operationalization of the framework and limited rigorous outcome evidence.
  - **Result:** Important early systematic examination of the UDL evidence base; commonly cited counterweight to enthusiastic adoption narratives.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0007

## A1.2 The effectiveness of universal design for learning: a meta-analysis of literature between 2013 and 2016

- **The effectiveness of universal design for learning: a meta-analysis of literature between 2013 and 2016** — Capp (2017); *International Journal of Inclusive Education, 21(8)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/13603116.2017.1325074
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Meta-analysis of 18 pre/post-test empirical studies (2013-2016) on UDL implementations; concludes UDL is effective for improving the learning process for all students but evidence on educational outcomes is not yet demonstrated.
  - **Result:** First aggregate quantitative evidence base for UDL; the standard citation when discussing UDL effect-size claims and their limits.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0006

## A1.4 About the UDL Guidelines 3.0 Update

  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Documents the July 2024 Guidelines 3.0 release: shifts from educator- to learner-centered language, integrates asset-based pedagogies, centers learner identity and systemic barriers, and reframes the UDL goal as learner agency.

## A1.5 Universal Design for Learning: Theory and Practice

- **Universal Design for Learning: Theory and Practice** — Meyer, Rose & Gordon (2014); *CAST Professional Publishing*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://www.cast.org/books-media/universal-design-for-learning-meyer-rose-gordon/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** First comprehensive book-length presentation of UDL principles and practice since 2002, integrating a decade of CAST research on learner variability and the three networks (recognition, strategic, affective) underlying the three UDL principles.
  - **Result:** Canonical book-length theoretical statement of UDL by its originators; the foundational reference cited by virtually every higher-ed UDL paper.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0004

## A1.6 The effectiveness of universal design for learning: a systematic review of the literature and meta-analysis

- **The effectiveness of universal design for learning: a systematic review of the literature and meta-analysis** — Mngo et al. (2023); *Cogent Education, 10(1)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2331186X.2023.2218191
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 peer-reviewed UDL studies (2015-2021) across six countries; reports statistically significant effects for student participants and specific-domain applications.
  - **Result:** Most recent aggregate effect-size estimate for UDL interventions; updates Capp (2017) with post-2016 evidence.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0009

## A1.7 Universal Design for Learning in Postsecondary Education: Reflections on Principles and their Application

- **Universal Design for Learning in Postsecondary Education: Reflections on Principles and their Application** — Rose, Harbour, Johnston, Daley & Abarbanell (2006); *Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability*. [domain_application]
  - **Source:** https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ844630.pdf
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reflects on how the three UDL principles (multiple means of representation, expression, engagement) play out in an actual Harvard graduate-level course, with examples of course goals, materials, methods, and assessment redesigned via UDL.
  - **Result:** Seminal article translating UDL from K-12 origins to higher ed practice; widely cited as the foundational postsecondary UDL reference.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0005

## A1.8 Transforming higher education: a systematic review of faculty training in UDL and its benefits

- **Transforming higher education: a systematic review of faculty training in UDL and its benefits** — Tlili et al. (2025); *Teaching in Higher Education (2025)*. [domain_application]
  - **Source:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13562517.2025.2465994
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Systematic review of 20 studies (363 faculty, 5,293 students) on UDL faculty-training programs in higher ed; finds targeted UDL training increases teaching competence and supports inclusive pedagogical practice.
  - **Result:** Most recent higher-ed-specific synthesis on UDL implementation via faculty development; directly addresses the postsecondary scope of the dossier.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_multi_paradigm_presentation_0010
