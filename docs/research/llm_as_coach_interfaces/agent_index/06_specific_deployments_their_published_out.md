## A6.5 The Widening Gap: The Benefits and Harms of Generative AI for Novice Programmers

- **The Widening Gap: The Benefits and Harms of Generative AI for Novice Programmers** — Prather et al. (2024); *ICER 2024*. [risk]
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17739
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Across 21 lab sessions (observation, interview, eye tracking), students with strong metacognition accelerated while struggling students saw difficulties compounded, surfacing new metacognitive failure modes (Interruption, Mislead, Progression) and an illusion of competence.
  - **Result:** Primary CS-education deployment evidence on uneven coding-tutor outcomes for novices.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0036

## A6.10 Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling Real-Time Expertise

- **Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling Real-Time Expertise** — Wang et al. (2024); *arXiv (Stanford, Oct 2024)*. [deployment]
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03017
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** First RCT of a Human-AI system in live tutoring (900 tutors, ~1,800 K-12 students); students of CoPilot-equipped tutors were 4pp more likely to master topics (9pp for lower-rated tutors), with tutors more likely to ask guiding questions and less likely to give away answers.
  - **Result:** Landmark 900-tutor RCT deployment with measured mastery gains and message-level pedagogical analysis.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0032
