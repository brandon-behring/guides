# Intelligent tutoring systems (ITS) historical foundations

Sub-area `A1` of the Research Plan: LLM-as-coach interfaces for technical learning dossier. 7 entries.

## A1.1 Cognitive Tutors: Lessons Learned

- **Cognitive Tutors: Lessons Learned** — Anderson, Corbett, Koedinger & Pelletier (1995); *The Journal of the Learning Sciences, 4(2), 167-207*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327809jls0402_2
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reviews a decade of ACT-based tutor development; best-case evaluations show students reaching the same proficiency as conventional instruction in one-third of the time, with immediate directed feedback the most effective tutorial style.
  - **Result:** Defining methodological synthesis of the cognitive-tutor / model-tracing paradigm and its design principles.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0006

## A1.2 The 2 Sigma Problem: The Search for Methods of Group Instruction as Effective as One-to-One Tutoring

- **The 2 Sigma Problem: The Search for Methods of Group Instruction as Effective as One-to-One Tutoring** — Bloom (1984); *Educational Researcher, 13(6), 4-16*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X013006004
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** The average student tutored one-to-one with mastery learning performed two standard deviations above the control class (above 98% of students); finding group-instruction methods as effective as tutoring is the 2 sigma problem.
  - **Result:** Foundational benchmark motivating the entire personalized-tutoring and LLM-coach research program.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0005

## A1.3 Knowledge Tracing: Modeling the Acquisition of Procedural Knowledge

- **Knowledge Tracing: Modeling the Acquisition of Procedural Knowledge** — Corbett & Anderson (1995); *User Modeling and User-Adapted Interaction, 4(4), 253-278*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01099821
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Introduces knowledge tracing, maintaining a probability estimate that the student has learned each production rule in an ideal student model, demonstrated in the ACT Programming Tutor for short-program writing.
  - **Result:** Origin of Bayesian knowledge tracing, the canonical student-modeling method underpinning adaptive tutoring.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0007

## A1.4 Intelligent Tutoring Goes To School in the Big City

- **Intelligent Tutoring Goes To School in the Big City** — Koedinger, Anderson, Hadley & Mark (1997); *International Journal of AI in Education, 8, 30-43*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1184/R1/6470153.v1
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** A new algebra curriculum with an embedded Algebra Cognitive Tutor dramatically enhanced learning; experimental students outperformed comparison classes by 15% on standardized tests and 100% on targeted-objective tests.
  - **Result:** Landmark field deployment establishing real-classroom efficacy of model-tracing cognitive tutors.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0004

## A1.5 Effectiveness of Intelligent Tutoring Systems: A Meta-Analytic Review

- **Effectiveness of Intelligent Tutoring Systems: A Meta-Analytic Review** — Kulik & Fletcher (2016); *Review of Educational Research, 86(1), 42-78*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315581420
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Meta-analysis of 50 controlled evaluations; median effect of intelligent tutoring raised test scores 0.66 SD, with effect size strongly moderated by whether tests were locally developed or standardized.
  - **Result:** Most-cited ITS effectiveness meta-analysis; complements vanlehn2011relative with a different sample and test-alignment caveat.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0009

## A1.6 The Behavior of Tutoring Systems

- **The Behavior of Tutoring Systems** — VanLehn (2006); *International Journal of AI in Education, 16(3), 227-265*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1435351.1435353
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Frames tutoring systems as two loops: an outer loop selecting tasks and an inner loop giving step-level feedback, hints, and updating a student model used to choose the next task.
  - **Result:** Provides the inner-loop/outer-loop vocabulary now used to compare LLM-coach interaction designs.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0008

## A1.7 The Relative Effectiveness of Human Tutoring, Intelligent Tutoring Systems, and Other Tutoring Systems

- **The Relative Effectiveness of Human Tutoring, Intelligent Tutoring Systems, and Other Tutoring Systems** — VanLehn (2011); *Educational Psychologist 46(4)*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2011.611369
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** VanLehn (2011) meta-analysis - ITS effect size d=0.76, human tutoring d=0.79.
  - **Result:** ITS nearly matches human tutoring effectiveness. Foundational for LLM-tutor expectations.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0001
