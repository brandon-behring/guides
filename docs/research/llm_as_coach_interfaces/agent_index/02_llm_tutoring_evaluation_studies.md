## A2.2 AI tutoring can safely and effectively support students: An exploratory RCT in UK classrooms

- **AI tutoring can safely and effectively support students: An exploratory RCT in UK classrooms** — Jurenka et al. (2025); *arXiv (Google DeepMind + Eedi, 2025)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Exploratory RCT with 165 students across five UK secondary schools integrating LearnLM into supervised chat tutoring on the Eedi maths platform; students supported by LearnLM performed at least as well as those with human tutors alone, with supervising tutors approving most LearnLM-drafted messages with zero or minimal edits.
  - **Result:** Human-in-the-loop LearnLM RCT in real classrooms — a rigorous deployment-grade tutoring evaluation; pairs against Kestin.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0015

## A2.3 AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting

- **AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting** — Kestin, Miller et al. (2025); *Scientific Reports 15:17458 (2025)*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** A randomized controlled trial in an authentic physics course found students learn more in less time when content is delivered through a pedagogically-designed GPT-4 AI tutor compared with in-class active learning; students were also more engaged and motivated.
  - **Result:** Canonical AI-tutoring-outperforms-active-learning RCT in a real Harvard physics course — the load-bearing positive-evidence anchor.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0014

## A2.5 Artificial intelligence in education: The three paradigms

- **Artificial intelligence in education: The three paradigms** — Ouyang & Jiao (2021); *Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 2:100020 (2021)*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2021.100020
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Proposes three AIEd paradigms — AI-directed (knowledge models direct cognition), AI-supported (learner as collaborator), and AI-empowered (learner takes agency) — as a framing lens for AI-in-education research.
  - **Result:** Most-cited Ouyang framing review; supplies the AI-directed/supported/empowered taxonomy for tutoring modalities.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_llm_as_coach_interfaces_0018
