# Documentation Methodology — OER Adoption and Sustainability

Studies and case reports on what makes Open Educational Resources actually get adopted (vs sitting in a repository unused) and what sustains them over time. Relevant to the public-guides repo's realistic expectations about reach and the design choices that aid adoption.
