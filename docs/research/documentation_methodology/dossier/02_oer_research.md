# Documentation Methodology — Open Educational Resources (Research Literature)

Peer-reviewed and foundation-published research on Open Educational Resources: efficacy meta-analyses, the 5R framework definition, and policy frameworks. This sub-area informs the open-licensing posture and attribution-by-design choices for the public-guides repo.

## B1. Efficacy meta-analyses

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Open educational resources and college textbook choices: a review of research on efficacy and perceptions | Hilton (2016) | Educational Technology Research and Development 64 | https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9434-9 | — | Literature review aggregating studies of OER replacing traditional textbooks in college courses, summarizing student outcome and perception findings. | Field's first major efficacy meta-review of OER vs traditional textbooks across multiple US college courses. |
| Open educational resources, student efficacy, and user perceptions: a synthesis of research published between 2015 and 2018 | Hilton (2020) | Educational Technology Research and Development 68 | https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09700-4 | — | Follow-up synthesis covering the 2015–2018 OER research window, updating efficacy and perception findings from the 2016 review. | Updated meta-synthesis covering the 2015–2018 OER research literature window. |
