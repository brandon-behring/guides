# Documentation Methodology — Open Educational Resources (Research Literature)

Peer-reviewed and foundation-published research on Open Educational Resources: efficacy meta-analyses, the 5R framework definition, and policy frameworks. Informs the open-licensing posture and attribution-by-design choices for the public-guides repo.

## B1. Efficacy meta-analyses

- **Open educational resources and college textbook choices: a review of research on efficacy and perceptions** — Hilton (Educational Technology Research and Development 64, 2016).
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9434-9
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Literature review aggregating studies of OER replacing traditional textbooks in college courses; summarizes findings on student outcomes (course grades, completion) and perceptions (student and faculty) across the studies surveyed.
  - **Result:** Field's first major efficacy meta-review of OER vs traditional textbooks across US college courses; widely cited in subsequent OER policy and adoption work.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Open educational resources, student efficacy, and user perceptions: a synthesis of research published between 2015 and 2018** — Hilton (Educational Technology Research and Development 68, 2020).
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09700-4
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Follow-up synthesis to the 2016 review, covering OER efficacy and perception research published 2015–2018; updates findings on student outcomes and perceptions with the newer evidence.
  - **Result:** Updated meta-synthesis covering the 2015–2018 OER research literature window.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

## B2. Definitions and policy frameworks

- **Defining the "Open" in Open Content and Open Educational Resources** — Wiley (opencontent.org, n.d.; 5R framework, 5th R added 2014).
  - **Source:** https://opencontent.org/definition/
  - **Status:** Unverified (website / ongoing).
  - **Mechanism:** Defines openness in educational content along five permissions — Retain, Reuse, Revise, Remix, Redistribute — collectively termed the 5R framework.
  - **Integration:** The 5R framework is the canonical permissions vocabulary for OER licensing decisions; the public-guides repo's CC BY 4.0 posture grants all 5Rs (with attribution).

- **Twenty years of Open Educational Resources: Building robust networks for innovation** — Hewlett Foundation (William and Flora Hewlett Foundation, 2022).
  - **Source:** https://hewlett.org/twenty-years-of-open-educational-resources-building-robust-networks-for-innovation/
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
  - **Mechanism:** Foundation retrospective covering twenty years of OER funding strategy, network-building efforts, and ecosystem development at a primary funder of the OER movement.
  - **Integration:** Funder-side history useful as context on which OER initiatives are institutionally sustained vs grant-dependent.

- **Recommendation on Open Educational Resources (OER)** — UNESCO (UNESCO General Conference 40th Session, 2019).
  - **Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-educational-resources-oer
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
  - **Mechanism:** UNESCO member-state recommendation establishing OER policy goals across five action areas — capacity building, supportive policy, equitable access, sustainability models, international cooperation.
  - **Integration:** Intergovernmental policy framework legitimizing OER as a global public-policy goal; useful as upstream policy justification when discussing OER with institutional stakeholders.
