# Documentation Methodology — OER Adoption and Sustainability

Studies and case reports on what makes Open Educational Resources actually get adopted (vs sitting in a repository unused) and what sustains them over time. Informs realistic expectations about reach and the design choices that aid adoption in the public-guides repo.

## C1. Adoption surveys and faculty perceptions

- **Student and Faculty Perceptions of OpenStax in High Enrollment Courses** — Pitt et al. (International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 2017).
  - **Source:** https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/2462
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Survey study of OpenStax open-textbook adoption in high-enrollment introductory college courses, capturing both faculty perceptions of quality and pedagogical fit and student perceptions of usefulness.
  - **Result:** Empirical adoption study of OpenStax in high-enrollment courses with both faculty and student perception data.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Eight Patterns of Open Textbook Adoption in British Columbia** — Jhangiani et al. (International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 2018).
  - **Source:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/326350980_Eight_Patterns_of_Open_Textbook_Adoption_in_British_Columbia
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Qualitative study identifying recurring adoption patterns observed in British Columbia faculty using BCcampus open textbooks, derived from faculty interviews and adoption records.
  - **Result:** Typology of faculty adoption patterns for open textbooks at the institutional level (British Columbia).
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

## C2. Platform sustainability case studies

- **Three Models for Sustaining Open Source Software: Pressbooks – Pooling Resources, Building Value** — BCcampus / Pressbooks (BCcampus SERT Case Studies, 2019).
  - **Source:** https://opentextbc.ca/sertcasestudies/chapter/pressbooks/
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
  - **Mechanism:** Case study analyzing the Pressbooks open-textbook publishing platform's sustainability model based on pooled institutional resources, contributor governance, and shared infrastructure.
  - **Integration:** Useful reference when designing the public-guides repo's sustainability model; the pooled-institutional-resources pattern is one of three documented sustainability paths.

- **Open Textbook Project turns two** — BCcampus (BCcampus, 2014).
  - **Source:** https://bccampus.ca/2014/10/16/open-textbook-project-2012-2014/
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
  - **Mechanism:** Two-year retrospective on the BCcampus Open Textbook Project covering catalog growth, faculty participation, and student savings since project launch in 2012.
  - **Integration:** Provincial-program retrospective useful as a model for how publicly-funded open-textbook initiatives report progress; not directly applicable to the public-guides repo's solo-maintainer model.
