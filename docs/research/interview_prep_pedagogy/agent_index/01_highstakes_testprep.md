# High-Stakes Credentialing Test-Prep Methodology

Industry-resource synthesis on CFA, SOA actuarial, and USMLE study cadence — pass-rate baselines, the multi-exam credentialing ladder, and high-yield content curation. These are practitioner vendor resources (no peer review). Empirical research on technical interviews lives in `02_interview_research.md`; spaced-repetition evidence specific to credentialing contexts lives in `03_spaced_repetition_credentialing.md`.

## A1. CFA / Actuarial / USMLE prep-method industry references

- **CFA pass-rate tracker** — 300Hours (2026, 300hours.com).
  - **Source:** https://300hours.com/cfa-pass-rates/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Aggregates reported CFA Level I / II / III pass rates across historical exam windows on a single practitioner-facing page.
  - **Result:** Provides the pass-rate baseline that candidates use to calibrate hours-to-target and per-Level difficulty expectations; cited when discussing why CFA candidates need substantial hours per Level and why the vignette-heavy Level II/III formats demand a different preparation rhythm than Level I.
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) (industry resource) Unverified.

- **SOA actuarial exam path guide** — Coaching Actuaries (coachingactuaries.com).
  - **Source:** https://www.coachingactuaries.com/blog/study-tips/a-comprehensive-guide-to-the-different-actuarial-exams-and-how-to-prepare-for-each
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Vendor-published walkthrough of the SOA exam ladder (P, FM, FAM, ALTAM, ASTAM, etc.) with per-exam study cadence guidance.
  - **Result:** Maps the multi-exam actuarial credentialing rhythm where candidates spend months on one exam at a time using question-bank-driven preparation; reference for how spaced multi-exam credentialing rhythms compare to single-shot technical-interview prep.
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) (industry resource) Unverified.

- **USMLE Step 1 high-yield topics** — The Match Guy (2026, thematchguy.com).
  - **Source:** https://thematchguy.com/high-yield-usmle-step-1-topics-you-must-know/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Practitioner-oriented summary of the topics that high-yield Step 1 study programs (First Aid, UWorld) prioritize for tested-content coverage.
  - **Result:** Captures the "high-yield curation" framing that USMLE prep uses — selecting heavily-tested content over comprehensive textbook coverage; reference for the curation-over-coverage tradeoff in technical-interview prep design.
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) (industry resource) Unverified.

**Notes:** All three are industry resources, not peer-reviewed studies. Specific pass-rate numbers from 300Hours and high-yield topic lists from The Match Guy should be re-checked against primary exam-vendor publications (CFA Institute statistics, NBME publications) before being asserted in synthesis material as factual claims.
