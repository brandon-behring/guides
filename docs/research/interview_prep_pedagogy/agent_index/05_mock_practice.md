# Mock-Practice Methodology and Think-Aloud Protocols

Industrial-organizational psychology on employment-interview validity, the academic ancestry of STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result) via behavior-description interviewing, and the methodological canon for verbal protocols as data (Ericsson & Simon). Classical problem-solving theory lives in `04_expert_problemsolving.md`; communication / signaling under time pressure lives in `06_signaling_communication.md`.

## E1. Behavior-description interviewing and STAR ancestry

- **Behavior Description Interviewing** — Janz, Hellervik & Gilmore (Allyn and Bacon 1986).
  - **Source:** https://archive.org/details/behaviordescript00janz
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Book-length treatment of behavior-description interviewing as a structured-interview methodology asking candidates to recount specific past behaviors in structured detail.
  - **Result:** Intellectual ancestor of the STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result) format used in behavioral interviews at top tech companies — STAR is essentially behavior-description-interviewing operationalized for self-report.
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) Unverified.

## E2. Validity of employment interviews

- **The Validity of Employment Interviews: A Comprehensive Review and Meta-Analysis** — McDaniel, Whetzel, Schmidt & Maurer (J. Applied Psychology 1994).
  - **Source:** https://home.ubalt.edu/tmitch/645/articles/McDanieletal1994CriterionValidityInterviewsMeta.pdf
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Meta-analysis aggregating studies on the criterion validity of employment interviews across structured / unstructured and content / situational variants.
  - **Result:** Canonical I-O psychology meta-analysis establishing that structured interviews outperform unstructured interviews for predicting job performance (specific effect sizes are in the paper body — unverified body claim).
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) Unverified.

## E3. Verbal protocols as data

- **Protocol Analysis: Verbal Reports as Data (rev. ed.)** — Ericsson & Simon (MIT Press 1993; rev. of 1984 ed.).
  - **Source:** https://www.ida.liu.se/~nilda08/Anders_Ericsson/Ericsson_protocol.pdf
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Methodological treatise on collecting and analyzing think-aloud verbal protocols as a window into cognitive processes during problem solving.
  - **Result:** Canonical reference for think-aloud as a research method and (downstream) as a pedagogical lever — verbalizing during problem solving is both data and intervention; the methodological foundation for "say what you are thinking" callouts in interview prep.
  - **Status:** (no widely-known repo) Unverified.

---

**Notes:** All three entries are academic. Janz et al. (1986) and Ericsson & Simon (1993) are books with stable bibliographic facts. McDaniel et al. (1994) is the J. Applied Psychology meta-analysis that anchors structured-interview validity claims; meta-analytic effect sizes should be re-verified against the primary PDF before being asserted in synthesis material.
