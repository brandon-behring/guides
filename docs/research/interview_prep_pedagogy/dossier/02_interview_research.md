# Empirical Research on Technical Interviews

Peer-reviewed empirical studies on technical interview formats — whiteboarding stress, cognitive load, what interviewers expect from candidates, and developers' reported experiences with the format. Industry credentialing-prep methodology lives in `01_highstakes_testprep.md`; mock-practice / behavior-description-interviewing methodology lives in `05_mock_practice.md`.

## B1. Cognitive-load and stress under whiteboard format

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Dazed: Measuring the Cognitive Load of Solving Technical Interview Problems at the Whiteboard | Behroozi, Lui, Moore, Ford, & Parnin (2018) | ICSE NIER 2018 | (no arXiv) | — | New-ideas-and-emerging-results paper proposing instrumentation to measure cognitive load during whiteboard coding interviews | Early empirical framing of whiteboard interviews as a cognitive-load measurement problem; sets up the line of work continued in Behroozi 2020 |
| Does Stress Impact Technical Interview Performance? | Behroozi, Shirolkar, Barik, & Parnin (2020) | ESEC/FSE 2020 | DOI:10.1145/3368089.3409712 | — | Empirical comparison of candidates solving problems on a whiteboard with an observer versus solving the same problems privately | Provides empirical evidence that the whiteboard-with-observer format depresses measured problem-solving performance relative to a private format |

## B2. Interviewer expectations and developer experience reports

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| The Tech-Talk Balance: What Technical Interviewers Expect from Technical Candidates | Ford, Barik, Rand-Pickett, & Parnin (2017) | IEEE/ACM CHASE 2017 | (no arXiv) | — | Interview-based study of what technical interviewers explicitly look for during candidate-interviewer technical conversations | Surfaces the "tech-talk" dimension — that interviews assess interactive technical communication, not only solution correctness |
