# Mock-Practice Methodology and Think-Aloud Protocols

Industrial-organizational psychology on employment-interview validity, the academic ancestry of the STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result) format via behavior-description interviewing, and the methodological canon for verbal protocols as data (Ericsson & Simon). Classical problem-solving theory lives in `04_expert_problemsolving.md`; communication / signaling under time pressure lives in `06_signaling_communication.md`.

## E1. Behavior-description interviewing and STAR ancestry

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Behavior Description Interviewing: New, Accurate, Cost Effective | Janz, Hellervik, & Gilmore (1986) | Allyn and Bacon | (no arXiv) | — | Book-length treatment of behavior-description interviewing as a structured-interview methodology | Intellectual ancestor of the STAR format — behavior-description interviewing asks candidates to recount specific past behaviors in structured detail |

## E2. Validity of employment interviews

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| The Validity of Employment Interviews: A Comprehensive Review and Meta-Analysis | McDaniel, Whetzel, Schmidt, & Maurer (1994) | Journal of Applied Psychology 79 | (no arXiv) | — | Meta-analysis aggregating studies on the criterion validity of employment interviews across structured / unstructured and content / situational variants | Canonical I-O psychology meta-analysis establishing that structured interviews outperform unstructured interviews for predicting job performance |

## E3. Verbal protocols as data

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Protocol Analysis: Verbal Reports as Data (Revised Edition) | Ericsson & Simon (1993; rev. of 1984 ed.) | MIT Press | (no arXiv) | — | Methodological treatise on collecting and analyzing think-aloud verbal protocols as a window into cognitive processes during problem solving | Canonical reference for think-aloud as a method and (downstream) as a pedagogical lever — verbalizing during problem solving is both data and intervention |
