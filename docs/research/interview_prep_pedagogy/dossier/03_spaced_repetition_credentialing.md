# Spaced Repetition + Testing Effect in Credentialing Contexts

Peer-reviewed evidence on spaced repetition, retrieval practice, and Anki-style flashcards in professional credentialing — what works, when, and why. Industry credentialing methodology lives in `01_highstakes_testprep.md`; classical problem-solving theory (Pólya / Schoenfeld) lives in `04_expert_problemsolving.md`.

## C1. Foundational learning-techniques and testing-effect evidence

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology | Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan, & Willingham (2013) | Psychological Science in the Public Interest 14(1) | DOI:10.1177/1529100612453266 | — | Comprehensive review classifying ten widely-used study techniques by utility level | Foundational synthesis identifying practice testing (retrieval practice) and distributed practice as the two high-utility techniques most other techniques pale next to |
| The Power of Testing Memory: Basic Research and Implications for Educational Practice | Roediger & Karpicke (2006) | Perspectives on Psychological Science 1(3) | (no arXiv) | — | Review article summarizing experimental evidence on the testing effect (retrieval practice strengthens memory more than restudy) | Canonical review establishing the testing effect as a robust and educationally important phenomenon |
