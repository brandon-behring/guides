# Expert Problem-Solving and Pattern Recognition

Classical and contemporary works on how experts solve problems and how novices acquire expert-like pattern recognition — Pólya's heuristic, Schoenfeld's metacognition framework, CS-education evidence on novice programmers, and the industry-canon problem-pattern book that downstream tech-interview prep is built on. Empirical interview research lives in `02_interview_research.md`; mock-practice / verbalization methodology lives in `05_mock_practice.md`.

## D1. Classical problem-solving theory

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| How to Solve It: A New Aspect of Mathematical Method | Pólya (1945; 2nd ed. 1957) | Princeton University Press | (no arXiv) | — | Book-length exposition of a four-phase problem-solving heuristic (understand the problem, devise a plan, carry out the plan, look back) | Foundational four-step heuristic that downstream CS-interview frameworks (clarify → plan → code → verify) descend from |
| Mathematical Problem Solving | Schoenfeld (1985) | Academic Press | (no arXiv) | — | Research monograph extending Pólya with empirical investigation of metacognition, heuristics, beliefs, and control in expert problem solving | Establishes metacognition and beliefs about problem-solving as load-bearing components of expert performance, beyond Pólya's heuristic alone |

## D2. CS-education evidence and industry-canon prep

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Studying the Novice Programmer | Soloway & Spohrer (eds.) (1989) | Lawrence Erlbaum Associates | (no arXiv) | — | Edited volume of empirical CS-education studies on how novice programmers acquire and structure programming knowledge | Establishes the empirical basis for chunking and plan-based pattern recognition as the mechanism by which novices grow into expert programmers |
| Cracking the Coding Interview: 189 Programming Questions and Solutions | McDowell (2015; 6th ed.) | CareerCup | (no arXiv) | — | Industry-canon self-published book of curated coding-interview problems organized by topic with worked solutions | Defines the dominant practitioner-facing pattern taxonomy (data-structure-by-data-structure organization) that contemporary tech-interview prep is built around |
