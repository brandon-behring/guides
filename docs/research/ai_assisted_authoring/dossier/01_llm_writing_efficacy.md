# LLM-Assisted Writing Efficacy — Empirical Productivity & Quality Studies

This file covers empirical evidence on whether LLM-assisted writing produces faster, better, or different output than unassisted writing. Disclosure norms live in `02_disclosure_norms.md`; human-in-the-loop interface patterns live in `03_hitl_patterns.md`.

## A1. Field & Randomized Productivity Studies

| Title | Authors (year) | Venue | arXiv/DOI | GitHub | One-line description | Key contribution |
|-------|----------------|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence | Noy & Zhang (2023) | Science | DOI:10.1126/science.adh2586 | — | Randomized controlled trial comparing ChatGPT-assisted to unassisted writers on midlevel professional writing tasks | Foundational randomized study showing substantial time reduction and quality lift on mid-level writing, with larger gains for less-skilled writers |
| The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot | Peng et al. (2023) | arXiv preprint | arXiv:2302.06590 | — | Randomized experiment measuring task-completion speed for developers using GitHub Copilot versus a control group | Reports significant developer-task time savings with Copilot; widely cited as the canonical Copilot productivity reference |
| Generative AI at Work | Brynjolfsson, Li, & Raymond (2023) | NBER Working Paper 31161 | DOI:10.3386/w31161 | — | Field study of generative-AI deployment in customer-support work using staggered rollout to identify productivity effects | First large-scale field-deployment evidence; finds gains concentrated in less-experienced workers, narrowing the experience gap |
| Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality | Dell'Acqua et al. (2023) | HBS Working Paper / SSRN | DOI:10.2139/ssrn.4573321 | — | Field experiment at a consulting firm comparing GPT-4-assisted to unassisted consultants across in-frontier and outside-frontier tasks | Introduces the "jagged frontier" framing — AI helps inside its capability frontier and harms accuracy on tasks outside it |
