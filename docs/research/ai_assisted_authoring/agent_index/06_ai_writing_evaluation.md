# AI-Writing Evaluation, Detection & Watermarking — Synthesis

Evaluation methodology and detection/watermarking tooling for AI-assisted writing. Source: dossier `06_ai_writing_evaluation.md`.

## F1. Blind-Evaluation & Domain Studies

- **Gao et al.** — "Comparing scientific abstracts generated by ChatGPT to original abstracts" (bioRxiv 2022, later npj Digital Medicine 2023).
  - **Source:** https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.12.23.521610v1
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Compares ChatGPT-generated medical abstracts to original published abstracts using a tripartite evaluation: an AI-output detector, a plagiarism detector, and blinded human medical-domain reviewers.
  - **Result:** Foundational blind-evaluation study showing reviewers can flag some AI-generated abstracts but also rate them as plausible — sets up the quality-vs-detectability tension that frames the disclosure-and-evaluation debate.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Liang et al.** — "Monitoring AI-Modified Content at Scale" (ICML 2024).
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07183
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Statistical estimation of the fraction of LLM-modified text in submitted peer reviews at major AI conferences using a distributional comparison method rather than per-document classification.
  - **Result:** Demonstrates a population-level estimation approach distinct from per-document detection; provides evidence of measurable LLM-modified content in academic peer review and a methodology applicable to large corpora.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

## F2. Detection & Watermarking Methods

- **DetectGPT** — Mitchell et al. (ICML 2023).
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11305
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Zero-shot detection exploiting the observation that model-generated text sits near local maxima of the source model's log-probability surface, while human-written text does not.
  - **Result:** Canonical zero-shot detection reference using probability-curvature signals; widely cited baseline for follow-on detection work. Detection reliability drops as models grow and as adversarial paraphrasing is introduced.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **A Watermark for Large Language Models** — Kirchenbauer et al. (ICML 2023).
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10226
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Embeds a statistical watermark at generation time by biasing the sampling distribution toward a pseudorandom "green list" of tokens at each step, detectable via a hypothesis test on token frequencies.
  - **Result:** Foundational LLM-watermarking paper introducing the green-list / red-list sampling approach that became the reference method for proactive provenance signals on machine-generated text.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
