# LLM-Assisted Writing Efficacy — Synthesis

Empirical evidence on whether LLM-assisted writing changes speed, quality, or workforce distribution of output. Source: dossier `01_llm_writing_efficacy.md`.

---

## A1. Field & Randomized Productivity Studies

- **Noy & Zhang (2023)** — "Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence" (Science, 2023).
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Randomized controlled trial assigning ChatGPT access to one arm of professional writers performing realistic mid-level writing tasks (press releases, short reports, analysis plans) and comparing outcomes to an unassisted control.
  - **Result:** Foundational randomized study showing substantial time reduction on mid-level writing with quality lift and a narrowing of the skill gap between less- and more-experienced writers. Widely cited as the anchor reference for "does LLM-assisted writing actually save time?"
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Peng et al. (2023)** — "The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot" (arXiv, 2023).
  - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Randomized field experiment in which developers complete a programming task with versus without GitHub Copilot, measuring time-to-completion as the primary outcome.
  - **Result:** Reports significant developer time savings with Copilot on the studied task; canonical Copilot productivity reference cited in subsequent productivity-of-LLM-tools literature.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Brynjolfsson, Li, & Raymond (2023)** — "Generative AI at Work" (NBER Working Paper 31161, 2023).
  - **Source:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Field study using staggered deployment of a generative-AI assistant to customer-support agents at a large firm, exploiting the rollout for difference-in-differences identification of productivity effects.
  - **Result:** First large-scale field-deployment study; finds productivity gains concentrated among less-experienced workers, with the tool acting as a partial substitute for tacit experience.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).

- **Dell'Acqua et al. (2023)** — "Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier" (HBS Working Paper / SSRN, 2023).
  - **Source:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4573321
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Field experiment at a global consulting firm assigning GPT-4 access to one arm of consultants performing realistic tasks, with task selection designed to span the inside and outside of plausible AI capability.
  - **Result:** Introduces the "jagged technological frontier" framing — AI assistance produces gains on tasks inside its capability frontier and degrades accuracy on tasks outside it. Reference paper for thinking about task-AI fit in knowledge work.
  - **Status:** Unverified (no widely-known repo).
