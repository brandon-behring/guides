# Expertise Development and Learning Outcomes

This file covers two adjacent theoretical lines: the cognitive psychology of expert performance (deliberate practice, chunking, pattern recognition) and the formal specification of learning outcomes (revised Bloom's taxonomy). Together they answer "how do learners become experts?" and "how do we describe what learners should be able to do?"

## C1. Expertise Development and Deliberate Practice
