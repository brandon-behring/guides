# Authentic assessment + portfolio-based measurement

Sub-area `A6` of the Research Plan: Transfer evaluation methods for interview-prep guides dossier. 4 entries.

## A6.1 A Systems Approach to Educational Testing

- **A Systems Approach to Educational Testing** — Frederiksen & Collins (1989); *Educational Researcher, 18(9)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X018009027
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Defines systemic validity: a test is systemically valid if it induces instructional and learning-strategy changes that foster the very cognitive traits it purports to measure.
  - **Result:** Introduces systemic validity, directly relevant to whether authentic transfer assessments drive the learning they claim to certify.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0042

## A6.2 Validating the Interpretations and Uses of Test Scores

- **Validating the Interpretations and Uses of Test Scores** — Kane (2013); *Journal of Educational Measurement, 50(1)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** An argument-based approach lays out the chain of inferences and assumptions from test responses to score interpretations and uses, then evaluates the plausibility of each link.
  - **Result:** Provides the validity-argument framework for scrutinizing the inferential leap from authentic-task performance to transfer claims.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0040

## A6.3 Validity of Psychological Assessment: Validation of Inferences from Persons' Responses and Performances as Scientific Inquiry into Score Meaning

- **Validity of Psychological Assessment: Validation of Inferences from Persons' Responses and Performances as Scientific Inquiry into Score Meaning** — Messick (1995); *American Psychologist, 50(9)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.50.9.741
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reconceptualizes validity as a unified construct-based judgment that must include the value implications of score meaning and the social consequences of score use (consequential validity).
  - **Result:** Source of consequential validity, the lens for judging whether transfer inferences from authentic tasks are warranted.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0039

## A6.4 Sampling Variability of Performance Assessments

- **Sampling Variability of Performance Assessments** — Shavelson, Baxter & Gao (1993); *Journal of Educational Measurement, 30(3)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-3984.1993.tb00424.x
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Generalizability-theory analysis shows large task-sampling variability in performance assessments, so many tasks are needed to generalize dependably from observed performance to the broader domain.
  - **Result:** Brings generalizability theory to bear on whether authentic-task scores generalize, a core measurement constraint on transfer inference.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0041
