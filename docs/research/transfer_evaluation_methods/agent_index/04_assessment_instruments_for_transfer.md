# Assessment instruments for transfer

Sub-area `A4` of the Research Plan: Transfer evaluation methods for interview-prep guides dossier. 6 entries.

## A4.1 On the Structure of Educational Assessments

- **On the Structure of Educational Assessments** — Mislevy, Steinberg & Almond (2003); *Measurement: Interdisciplinary Research and Perspectives, 1(1), 3-62*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1207/S15366359MEA0101_02
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Lays out evidence-centered design (ECD), making explicit the student, evidence, and task models that connect what we want to infer (e.g., transfer) to observable task performance.
  - **Result:** Canonical Mislevy ECD source for principled construction of transfer-assessment tasks.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0014

## A4.2 Knowing What Students Know: The Science and Design of Educational Assessment

- **Knowing What Students Know: The Science and Design of Educational Assessment** — Pellegrino, Chudowsky & Glaser (eds.) (2001); *National Academies Press (NRC)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.17226/10019
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Frames assessment as an evidentiary assessment triangle (cognition, observation, interpretation), arguing instruments should target models of how knowledge develops and can be applied.
  - **Result:** The NRC report named in scope; foundational for designing transfer-sensitive assessment.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0013

## A4.3 Inventing to Prepare for Future Learning: The Hidden Efficiency of Encouraging Original Student Production in Statistics Instruction

- **Inventing to Prepare for Future Learning: The Hidden Efficiency of Encouraging Original Student Production in Statistics Instruction** — Schwartz & Martin (2004); *Cognition and Instruction, 22(2), 129-184*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci2202_1
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Introduces the double-transfer assessment that embeds a learning resource into the transfer test, revealing PFL gains from invention activities that a standard sequestered transfer test misses.
  - **Result:** Operationalizes the preparation-for-future-learning assessment design central to A4.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0015

## A4.4 The Role of Assessment in a Learning Culture

- **The Role of Assessment in a Learning Culture** — Shepard (2000); *Educational Researcher, 29(7), 4-14*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X029007004
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Contrasts behaviorist/measurement assessment with a social-constructivist framework, arguing classroom assessment should be dynamic and embedded to support transferable understanding.
  - **Result:** Connects assessment theory to situated/performance assessment design implications for transfer.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0017

## A4.5 Dynamic Testing: The Nature and Measurement of Learning Potential

- **Dynamic Testing: The Nature and Measurement of Learning Potential** — Sternberg & Grigorenko (2002); *Cambridge University Press*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2002-01422-000
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Surveys dynamic-testing approaches that measure learning potential during guided instruction rather than static prior achievement, a near-relative of PFL transfer assessment.
  - **Result:** Authoritative review of dynamic assessment, the instrument family named in A4 scope.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0016

## A4.6 A True Test: Toward More Authentic and Equitable Assessment

- **A True Test: Toward More Authentic and Equitable Assessment** — Wiggins (1989); *Phi Delta Kappan, 70(9), 703-713 (reprinted 2011)*. [assessment]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/003172171109200721
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Argues for authentic performance assessment that requires students to apply knowledge to realistic, contextualized tasks rather than recognize answers, foregrounding transfer of competence.
  - **Result:** Foundational case for authentic/performance assessment, the instrument type named in A4 scope.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0018
