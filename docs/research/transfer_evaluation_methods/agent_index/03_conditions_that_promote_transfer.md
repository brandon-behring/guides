## A3.1 Self-Regulated Learning: Beliefs, Techniques, and Illusions

- **Self-Regulated Learning: Beliefs, Techniques, and Illusions** — Bjork, Dunlosky & Kornell (2013); *Annual Review of Psychology, 64*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-113011-143823
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reviews desirable difficulties (spacing, interleaving, testing, varied practice) that slow apparent acquisition but enhance durable, transferable learning, and the metacognitive illusions that lead learners to avoid them.
  - **Result:** Authoritative synthesis framing the desirable-difficulties account that unifies the A3 conditions.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0038

## A3.2 Self-Explanations: How Students Study and Use Examples in Learning to Solve Problems

- **Self-Explanations: How Students Study and Use Examples in Learning to Solve Problems** — Chi, Bassok, Lewis, Reimann & Glaser (1989); *Cognitive Science, 13(2)*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Good learners generated many self-explanations while studying worked examples, refining the conditions for actions; poor learners did not, predicting later problem-solving success.
  - **Result:** Origin of the self-explanation effect as a transfer-promoting study activity.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0035

## A3.5 Structuring the Transition from Example Study to Problem Solving in Cognitive Skill Acquisition: A Cognitive Load Perspective

- **Structuring the Transition from Example Study to Problem Solving in Cognitive Skill Acquisition: A Cognitive Load Perspective** — Renkl & Atkinson (2003); *Educational Psychologist, 38(1)*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_3
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Gradually fading worked-out solution steps into problems-to-solve eases the example-to-problem transition and improves skill acquisition under a cognitive-load account.
  - **Result:** Canonical worked-example-fading design for scaffolding transfer.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0036

## A3.6 Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention

- **Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention** — Roediger & Karpicke (2006); *Psychological Science, 17(3)*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Free-recall testing without feedback produced substantially better one-week retention than equivalent restudy, establishing the testing/retrieval-practice effect with prose materials.
  - **Result:** Canonical demonstration of retrieval practice as a durable learning condition.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0033

## A3.7 The shuffling of mathematics problems improves learning

- **The shuffling of mathematics problems improves learning** — Rohrer & Taylor (2007); *Instructional Science, 35(6)*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-006-9002-5
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Interleaving (shuffling) practice problems of different types rather than blocking them by topic markedly improved performance on a delayed test, despite feeling harder during practice.
  - **Result:** Foundational empirical demonstration of interleaved practice as a transfer-promoting condition.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0031

## A3.8 Interleaving Helps Students Distinguish among Similar Concepts

- **Interleaving Helps Students Distinguish among Similar Concepts** — Rohrer (2012); *Educational Psychology Review, 24(3)*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-012-9201-3
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Argues interleaving improves learning chiefly by requiring discrimination among similar concepts, building the category knowledge that supports later transfer to novel items.
  - **Result:** Specifies the discrimination mechanism by which interleaving generalizes, distinct from spacing.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_transfer_evaluation_methods_0032
