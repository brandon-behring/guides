## A1.1 Authentic assessment of teaching in context

- **Authentic assessment of teaching in context** — Darling-Hammond & Snyder (2000); *Teaching and Teacher Education, 16(5-6), 523-545*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/S0742-051X%2800%2900015-9
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reviews how teacher-education programs use authentic assessments (cases, exhibitions, portfolios, action research) to support teacher learning, arguing context-embedded performance evidence is more valid for judging teaching than decontextualized tests.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0003

## A1.2 A Systems Approach to Educational Testing

- **A Systems Approach to Educational Testing** — Frederiksen & Collins (1989); *Educational Researcher, 18(9), 27-32*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X018009027
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Introduces systemic validity — tests are systemically valid when they induce curricular and instructional changes that foster the cognitive traits the test claims to measure; argues direct (not indirect) assessment is essential.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0008

## A1.3 A Five-Dimensional Framework for Authentic Assessment

- **A Five-Dimensional Framework for Authentic Assessment** — Gulikers, Bastiaens & Kirschner (2004); *Educational Technology Research and Development, 52(3), 67-86*. [methodology]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504676
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Proposes a five-dimensional framework (task, physical context, social context, assessment result/form, criteria) that operationalizes authenticity for assessment design, grounded in qualitative validation with vocational-nursing students and teachers.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0007

## A1.4 The Interplay of Evidence and Consequences in the Validation of Performance Assessments

- **The Interplay of Evidence and Consequences in the Validation of Performance Assessments** — Messick (1994); *Educational Researcher, 23(2), 13-23*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X023002013
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Treats authenticity and directness of performance assessment as promissory validity claims that offset the two major threats to construct validity (construct underrepresentation and construct-irrelevant variance), arguing authentic assessment is fundamentally a quest for complete construct representation.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0004

## A1.5 Authentic Pedagogy and Student Performance

- **Authentic Pedagogy and Student Performance** — Newmann, Marks & Gamoran (1996); *American Journal of Education, 104(4), 280-312*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1086/444136
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Empirical study across restructured elementary, middle, and high schools showing authentic pedagogy is strongly associated with authentic academic performance and that the achievement effects are reasonably equitable across student social backgrounds.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0005

## A1.6 Authentic Intellectual Work and Standardized Tests: Conflict or Coexistence?

- **Authentic Intellectual Work and Standardized Tests: Conflict or Coexistence?** — Newmann, Bryk & Nagaoka (2001); *Consortium on Chicago School Research*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://consortium.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/2023-06/Authentic%20Intellectual%20Work-Jan2001-Consortium.pdf
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Chicago classroom study showing assignments demanding more authentic intellectual work actually improve student scores on conventional standardized tests, refuting the assumption that authentic and standardized assessment are in conflict.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0006

## A1.8 Educative Assessment: Designing Assessments to Inform and Improve Student Performance

- **Educative Assessment: Designing Assessments to Inform and Improve Student Performance** — Wiggins (1998); *Jossey-Bass*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://archive.org/details/educativeassessm00wigg
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Wiggins argues school-based assessment should be designed primarily to improve, not audit, student performance: authentic tasks replicate the standards and contexts of adult work, while contrived tasks exist only for efficient grading.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0002
