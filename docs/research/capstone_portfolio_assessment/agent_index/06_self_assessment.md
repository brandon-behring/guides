## A6.2 A Critical Review of Research on Student Self-Assessment

- **A Critical Review of Research on Student Self-Assessment** — Andrade (2019); *Frontiers in Education, 4, 87*. [evidence]
  - **Source:** https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2019.00087
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Reviews 76 empirical studies (2013-2018) on student self-assessment, finding consistent positive associations with achievement and self-regulated learning but documenting accuracy limitations (especially for novice and low-achieving students) and student-perception caveats.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0047

## A6.3 Reflection: Turning Experience into Learning

## A6.4 Rethinking Assessment in Higher Education: Learning for the Longer Term

## A6.6 The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action

- **The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action** — Schon (1983); *Basic Books (1983); reissued by Routledge*. [theory]
  - **Source:** https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/mono/10.4324/9781315237473/reflective-practitioner-donald-sch%C3%B6n
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Distinguishes reflection-in-action (real-time reframing while working) from reflection-on-action (retrospective analysis), arguing that professional schools over-rely on technical rationality and underweight the tacit-knowledge work practitioners actually do.
  - **Result:** See Mechanism.
  - **Status:** Unverified
  - **Evidence:** ev_capstone_portfolio_assessment_0041
