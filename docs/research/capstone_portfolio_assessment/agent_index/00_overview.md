## Glossary

- **Authentic assessment**: assessment that replicates the standards and contexts of adult/professional work, in contrast to tasks designed only for efficient grading (Wiggins 1998).
- **Capstone**: a culminating course or experience where students integrate prior coursework into a sustained project or portfolio. AAC&U lists capstone courses/projects as one of 10 high-impact practices (Kuh 2008).
- **CBE (Competency-Based Education)**: programs that credential demonstrated mastery of competencies rather than time-in-seat; quality framework via C-BEN (2017).
- **ePortfolio**: an electronic collection of work-and-reflection used for assessment, professional formation, and/or integrative learning (Yancey, Cambridge, Penny Light & colleagues).
- **Construct validity**: the inference that test scores reflect the construct claimed; the two major threats are construct underrepresentation and construct-irrelevant variance (Messick 1994).
- **Cohen's kappa**: chance-corrected agreement coefficient for nominal rater scales (Cohen 1960). For ordinal/interval scales or >2 raters, prefer Krippendorff's alpha (Krippendorff 2004).
- **Programmatic assessment**: aggregating many low-stakes assessments into a high-stakes competency decision; argues against single-test credentialing (van der Vleuten et al. 2018).
- **Reflection-in-action vs reflection-on-action**: real-time reframing while working vs retrospective analysis after the fact (Schön 1983).
- **Self-regulated learning (SRL)**: three-phase cyclical model — forethought, performance, self-reflection — linking metacognition to subsequent strategy choice (Zimmerman 2002).
- **VALUE rubrics**: 16 AAC&U cross-cutting rubrics designed for ePortfolio-style direct assessment of essential learning outcomes.
