# Research Plan: Capstone + portfolio assessment design

How portfolio-style capstones and project-based assessments are designed and evaluated in technical / professional pedagogy. Underwrites the Phase 1.5 capstone work in `~/guides-experimentation/capstone/` + the prompt-injection-detection-prototype reference model. Target: ~60-100 entries across engineering-education, ePortfolios, professional-certification design, and authentic-assessment literature.

## Sub-areas

- A1. Authentic assessment + performance-based measurement
  - Source types: Educational Measurement (NCME), AERA Handbook of Educational Assessment, classic Wiggins/Newmann texts
  - Notes: IN — Wiggins' authentic assessment framework, performance-task design, validity claims for performance assessment. OUT — pure psychometric theory (IRT, factor analysis).

- A2. Capstone design in engineering + CS education
  - Source types: ASEE / ICER / SIGCSE proceedings, Journal of Engineering Education
  - Notes: IN — capstone design experiences, software engineering capstones, ABET-relevant capstone outcomes assessment. Direct domain match.

- A3. ePortfolios + portfolio assessment in higher ed
  - Source types: AAEEBL ePortfolio Review, Routledge handbooks on ePortfolios, accreditation literature
  - Notes: IN — ePortfolio structure, longitudinal assessment design, portfolio rubric design. OUT — K-12 portfolio practice.

- A4. Professional certification + competency-based assessment
  - Source types: certification body standards (e.g., AWS / Google ML certs documentation), competency-based credentialing literature
  - Notes: IN — competency frameworks, certification exam design, technical-skill assessment validation. Distinct authority structure from academic capstones.

- A5. Rubric design + interrater reliability
  - Source types: Educational Measurement journals, Educational Psychology Review
  - Notes: IN — analytic vs holistic rubrics, dimension + criterion design, interrater reliability methods. Cross-cutting infrastructure for all the other sub-areas.

- A6. Self-assessment + reflection as portfolio components
  - Source types: Higher Education, Studies in Higher Education, Reflective Practice journal
  - Notes: IN — reflective-writing prompts, self-evaluation alignment with external rubrics. **Cross-cite with `llm_as_coach_interfaces` dossier on LLM-coached self-assessment.**

## Out-of-scope

- K-12 portfolio assessment (different audience + accountability structure)
- High-stakes summative testing without portfolio components (different validity framework)
- Pure cognitive-load theory (sister `learning_pedagogy` dossier)
- LLM-coached self-assessment — owned by `llm_as_coach_interfaces` dossier; this one cites.

## Claim family taxonomy

- `theory` — frameworks for authentic + portfolio-based assessment
- `methodology` — actionable design moves (rubric construction, capstone scoping)
- `evidence` — empirical validity + reliability studies
- `domain_application` — engineering / CS / data-science specific findings
- `certification` — professional certification design as a distinct authority structure
- `pitfall` — known failure modes (rubric drift, halo effects, score inflation, hidden curriculum)

## Known landmark papers

- `wiggins1998educative` — Wiggins "Educative Assessment" (Jossey-Bass 1998). Canonical authentic-assessment reference.
- `darling2008assessment` — Darling-Hammond & Snyder "Authentic assessment of teaching in context" (Teaching and Teacher Education 2000). Foundational for performance assessment.
- `kuh2008high` — Kuh "High-Impact Educational Practices" (AAC&U 2008). Capstones as a high-impact practice.
