# Figure-Text Integration Patterns

Synthesis of figure-text integration practice: when interactivity earns its complexity, what captioning conventions establish, and the historical foundation of hyperlinked reading. Coverage spans Distill's institutional position on clarity (Olah & Carter), Bret Victor's essays on explorable explanations and the abstraction ladder, and Bush's foundational Memex essay.

## C1. Distill and the institutional clarity bar

- **Research Debt** — Olah & Carter (Distill 2017).
  - **Source:** https://distill.pub/2017/research-debt/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Essay framing the cost imposed on readers when authors fail to invest in clarity; argues that distillation is undervalued and ought to be recognized as research work.
  - **Result:** Establishes the contemporary clarity bar for technical exposition; motivates the figure-as-explanation discipline that defines Distill-era articles and informs ML-blog standards.
  - **Status:** Unverified. (no widely-known repo)

- **About Distill** — Distill editorial team.
  - **Source:** https://distill.pub/about/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Editorial statement defining Distill's standards for interactive, clear, and reproducible machine-learning research exposition.
  - **Result:** Documents the editorial standards that adapted Tufte's print-design principles to interactive web-native ML articles; defines the bar for what a modern technical-explanation article is.
  - **Status:** Unverified. (no widely-known repo)

## C2. Explorable explanations and the abstraction ladder

- **Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction** — Victor (worrydream.com 2011).
  - **Source:** https://worrydream.com/LadderOfAbstraction/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Essay on building interactive simulations that let readers navigate concrete and abstract views of the same system; develops the abstraction-ladder pattern through a worked example.
  - **Result:** Canonical reference for explorable / interactive technical exposition; defines the abstraction-ladder pattern for showing concrete and abstract views together in a single interactive artifact.
  - **Status:** Unverified. (no widely-known repo)

- **Explorable Explanations** — Victor (worrydream.com 2011).
  - **Source:** https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Manifesto-style essay arguing that interactive explanations let readers act on ideas rather than passively consume them, and that this matters for understanding.
  - **Result:** Names and motivates the "explorable explanation" genre as a distinct mode of technical writing where readers manipulate the underlying model directly.
  - **Status:** Unverified. (no widely-known repo)

- **As We May Think** — Bush (The Atlantic Monthly 1945).
  - **Source:** https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/1945/07/as-we-may-think/303881/
  - **Code:** —
  - **Mechanism:** Foundational essay describing the Memex: a hypothetical hyperlinked information device for navigating recorded knowledge through associative trails.
  - **Result:** Foundational reference for hyperlinked / cross-referenced reading; precursor to the modern hypertext discipline that web-native technical books inherit.
  - **Status:** Unverified. (no widely-known repo)
