# Technical Book Design — Figure-Text Integration Patterns

Sources on figure-first vs text-first chapters, captioning conventions, the decision of when interactivity earns its complexity in book-pace artifacts, and the broader question of what a technical writer owes the reader. Primary sources span Distill's institutional position on clarity (Olah & Carter's "Research Debt"), Bret Victor's essays on explorable explanations and abstraction, and the historical foundation of hypertext-style reading (Bush's Memex).
